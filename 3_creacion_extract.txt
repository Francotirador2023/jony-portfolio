Project Proposal
CardioCare – Cardiac Monitoring Application
1. Project Name
CardioCare – Smart Cardiac Monitoring Application
2. Project Description
CardioCare is a mobile and web-based medical application designed to help doctors monitor the heart health of patients remotely. The system will collect cardiac data such as heart rate and rhythm through wearable devices and send the information directly to doctors through a secure digital platform.
This solution will allow doctors to detect potential cardiac problems early and provide faster medical assistance.
3. Objectives
General ObjectiveDevelop a digital medical platform that allows doctors to monitor patients' cardiac health in real time.
Specific Objectives
Improve communication between patients and doctors.
Provide real-time cardiac monitoring.
Reduce the risk of undetected heart problems.
Facilitate remote medical supervision.
4. Vision
To become a leading digital health solution in Peru that improves cardiac care through innovative medical technology.
5. Mission
To develop reliable and accessible healthcare technology that allows doctors to monitor patients' heart conditions efficiently and safely.
6. Target Users
Cardiologists

Patients with heart conditions
Hospitals and clinics
7. Main Features of the Product

Automatic alerts for abnormal heart activity

Data visualization and reports
